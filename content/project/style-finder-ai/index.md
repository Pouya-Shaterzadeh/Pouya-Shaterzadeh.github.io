AI fashion search pipeline. Llama 4 Maverick analyzes outfit photos, Fashion-CLIP finds visually similar products from Trendyol's catalog in ~5 seconds.

**How it works:**
- 📸 Upload a photo
- 🧠 Llama 4 Maverick analyzes the outfit
- 🔍 Fashion-CLIP re-ranks for visual similarity
- 🛍️ Returns matched Trendyol products
- ⚡ ~5 seconds end-to-end
